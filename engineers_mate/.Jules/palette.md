## 2024-05-13 - Tooltips as ARIA labels in Flutter
**Learning:** In Flutter applications, adding a `tooltip` property to an `IconButton` is essential for accessibility. It acts as an ARIA-equivalent accessibility label for screen readers while also providing visual hover context.
**Action:** Always include the `tooltip` property explicitly on icon-only buttons (`IconButton` widgets) to ensure both visual hover states and screen reader support are maintained across the application.
